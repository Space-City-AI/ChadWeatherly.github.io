01 / Research

Continual Learning

Anomaly detection under drift

Inspection models are usually judged on fixed test sets, but production lines change. This work measures which approaches stay reliable when they do. Published at ECCV 2026.

Self-Supervised Learning

Learning what normal looks like

Representations trained on unlabeled production data, so that rare and unseen defects stand out.

Edge AI

Inspection at the edge

Compact models that run on site, at line speed, without a data center behind them.
